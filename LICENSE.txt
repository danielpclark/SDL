sdl3-image/src/jxl/ is a translation of the JPEG XL decoder of libjxl
(https://github.com/libjxl/libjxl), at the revision SDL_image's
external/libjxl pins (libjxl 0.7.3 with SDL's patches,
2dac6356c045c0773b109b3f9ef67df208fca05b), an altered version of that
software, distributed under libjxl's license; libjxl's LICENSE and PATENTS
files follow verbatim. The individual files carry libjxl's copyright line.
libjxl is written with Highway (https://github.com/google/highway, its
third_party/highway, Highway 1.2.0), whose operations the translation
follows as Highway's scalar target implements them (one lane per vector);
Highway is dual-licensed under the Apache License 2.0 and the BSD 3-Clause
license, both of which follow verbatim too. libjxl's other dependencies
(brotli, skcms, lcms2) are not translated: the decoder SDL_image drives
needs no color management, and reads no brotli-compressed boxes.
